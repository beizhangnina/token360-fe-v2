v6 Brand Guidelines

A restrained, Stripe-inspired identity built around one accent color. Purple as punctuation, not prose. Warmth through neutral tones. Premium through restraint.

Version 6.0 — April 2026

Design Philosophy

Core Principle

Color is punctuation, not prose. Purple appears at ~5% of the page surface — in buttons, icon tints, small accent dots, and hover states. The remaining 95% is neutral warm tones. This creates a premium, trustworthy feel without the "AI startup" aesthetic.

What We Learned (v4 → v6)

VersionApproachResult
v4-blueBlue + Orange gradients everywhereGeneric AI startup look
v5-purplePurple + Teal gradientsStill too "AI-coded"
v5 monoPurple-only gradientsBetter, still gradient-heavy
v6 goldPurple + Gold dual accentTwo colors competed for attention
v6 finalPurple as sole accent, used sparinglyClean, premium, Stripe-level restraint ✓

Rules

  • No gradient text. Headings are always solid color — dark in light mode, warm-white in dark mode
  • No dot grid backgrounds. Clean solid colors only
  • No glass morphism. Cards use solid backgrounds with subtle borders
  • No emoji in badges. Text-only, neutral styling
  • One accent color. Purple #9633AF everywhere — no secondary accent competes
  • Purple only in small elements. Buttons, icon tints, dots, hover states, section accents
