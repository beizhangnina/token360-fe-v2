Usage Rules

Minimum Size

VariantMinimum Height
Icon mark24px (digital), 10mm (print)
Horizontal32px (digital), 12mm (print)
Vertical48px (digital), 18mm (print)

Clear Space

Minimum clear space around the logo equals the height of the letter "T" in the mark. No other elements should intrude into this space.

Background Rules

BackgroundVersion
White / Light (#FAF8F5)Use *-light variants
Dark (#1E1B22 or darker)Use *-dark variants
Photography / ComplexAdd white or dark container behind logo

Don'ts

  • Don't rotate or skew the logo
  • Don't change the colors of individual elements
  • Don't add drop shadows, outlines, or effects
  • Don't place on busy or low-contrast backgrounds without a container
  • Don't stretch or distort proportions
  • Don't separate the mark from the wordmark in horizontal/vertical variants
